One line, 91 characters: the excuse a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week. It starts as the most boring excuse there is — the car won't start — and then its own last words collapse it: the gamer left the car charging his controller all night for the midnight launch.

The entry:

> `Sorry, car won't start, I left it charging my controller all night for the midnight launch.`

91 characters, 16 words, one line, valid UTF-8 — inside the 140-character / 20-word gate in `RULES.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```

Writes the excuse to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and validates it against the gate (1–140 characters, at most 20 words, single line, non-whitespace, valid UTF-8) before and after writing.
